# 🏎️ Car Racing Game

A classic, browser-based 2D arcade car racing game built using HTML5 Canvas, CSS3, and JavaScript. Avoid oncoming traffic, collect coins to boost your score, and try to break your high score!

---

## 🚀 Features

* **HTML5 Canvas Animation:** Smooth gameplay driven by `requestAnimationFrame`.
* **Dynamic Gameplay:**
* Collect **Coins ($)** to gain **+50 bonus points**.
* Avoid enemy cars spawning across three lanes.
* Game speed increases progressively as your score climbs.


* **Life & High Score Tracking:**
* Start with **3 Lives (❤️❤️❤️)**.
* High scores persist locally via `localStorage`.


* **Responsive & Mobile Friendly:**
* Play using keyboard controls on desktop.
* Touch control buttons automatically appear on smaller screens/mobile devices.


* **Game Controls:** Built-in Pause and Restart buttons.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### Controls

* **Desktop:** Use **Arrow Left (⬅️)** and **Arrow Right (➡️)** keys to steer your car.
* **Mobile:** Tap the on-screen **⬅️** and **➡️️** buttons to steer.
* **UI Buttons:**
* **⏸️ Pause:** Pause or resume the current game session.
* **🔄 Restart:** Reset score, lives, and obstacle speeds to start fresh.



### Game Rules

1. **Dodge Traffic:** Hitting an enemy car loses **1 Life**. The game ends when all 3 lives are gone.
2. **Collect Coins:** Drive over coins to earn extra score points (+50 points each).
3. **Survive:** The longer you survive, the higher your score grows, and the faster the cars will move!
